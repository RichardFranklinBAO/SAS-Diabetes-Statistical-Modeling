# Output conventions

The verified original-data aggregate results are summarized in [RESULTS_COMPARISON.md](../RESULTS_COMPARISON.md); no separate aggregate CSV is published.

After running the public synthetic script locally, Stata creates a new synthetic-demo .log, a metrics .csv and .dta, and five figures (group boxplot, residuals vs fitted, Q–Q, adjusted probabilities, ROC). They are generated locally and are NOT pre-submitted as validated synthetic outputs.

Never publish full original-data logs, original observations, private diagnostic figures, or other participant-level outputs.
